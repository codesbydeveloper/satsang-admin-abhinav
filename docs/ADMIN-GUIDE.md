# Satsang Spark Admin Panel – Simple Guide

This guide is for Loyadham staff who look after Satsang Spark.

## 1. Opening the Admin Panel

1. Open your web browser (for example Chrome, Edge or Safari).
2. Go to: **<ADMIN_PANEL_LINK>**
3. Save this page as a bookmark, so you can find it again.

## 2. Logging in

Logging in has two steps.

**Step 1: Email and password**

1. Type your **Email** and **Password**.
2. Click **Continue**.

**Step 2: The code from your email**

1. We send a **6-digit code** to your email. The screen says **"Check your email"**.
2. Open your email and find the message **"Your Satsang Spark admin login code"**.
3. Type the 6 digits in the **Code** box. You are logged in as soon as you type the last digit. You can also click **Log in**.

Good to know:

- The code works for **10 minutes**.
- No email? Look in your **Spam** or **Junk** folder first.
- Still nothing? Wait one minute, then click **Resend code**. The button shows a countdown until you can use it.
- A new code replaces the old one. Always use the newest code.
- After 5 wrong codes, you go back to Step 1. Log in again.
- After too many wrong tries, your login is locked for 15 minutes. Wait, then try again.
- To begin again at any time, click **Start over**.

## 3. Roles: what each admin can do

There are two kinds of admin.

| | Full Admin | Content Editor |
|---|---|---|
| Dashboard | All numbers and charts | Welcome page only |
| Users (search, details, age group) | Yes | No |
| Deletion requests | Yes | No |
| Referrals | Yes | No |
| Blocked words | Yes | Yes |
| Admins (add and manage admins) | Yes | No |
| Audit log | Yes | No |
| My account (change your password) | Yes | Yes |

A Content Editor will also look after content (quizzes, stories and more) when those sections are added.

## 4. Adding a new admin (Full Admins only)

1. Click **Admins** in the menu.
2. Click **Add admin**.
3. Type their **Name** and **Email**. Choose their **Role**.
4. Click **Send invite**. You see **"Invite sent to …"**.
5. The new person gets an email with a link.
6. They open the link **within 48 hours**.
7. They choose a password (at least 12 characters), type it again to confirm, and click **Set password**.
8. They see **"Your account is ready"**. They click **Go to login** and log in as in section 2.

Until they finish, the list shows **Invited – expires (date)**.

- **Resend invite:** sends a new link for another 48 hours. The old link stops working.
- **Cancel invite:** removes the invite. The link stops working. You can invite them again later.

If the person's link has expired, they see "Ask a Full Admin to resend your invite." Click **Resend invite** for them.

## 5. Changing a role, deactivating and reactivating

In **Admins**, use the buttons on the right of each person:

- **Change role:** choose Full Admin or Content Editor, then click **Change role**. They are logged out and log in again with the new role.
- **Deactivate:** they are logged out at once and cannot log in. Their status shows **Inactive**.
- **Reactivate:** an inactive admin can log in again.

Rules that keep the panel safe:

- You cannot change your own role or deactivate yourself. Your row says **Your account**.
- There must always be at least one active Full Admin. The panel will not let you remove the last one.

## 6. Forgotten password

1. Ask a **Full Admin** for help.
2. The Full Admin opens **Admins** and clicks **Reset password** on your row.
3. They type a new temporary password and give it to you in a safe way (in person or by phone).
4. Log in with it. Then go to **My account → Change password** and choose your own password.

If you know your password and just want a new one, use **My account → Change password**. You are then logged out everywhere and log in again.

## 7. Deletion requests (Full Admins only)

People can ask for their account to be deleted on the website. These requests come here. A number next to **Deletion requests** in the menu shows how many are waiting.

The tabs:

- **Pending:** waiting for you.
- **Completed:** the account was deleted.
- **Rejected:** you said no, with a reason.
- **All:** everything.

To handle a request:

1. Click **View** to see the details, including any child profiles that will also be deleted.
2. To delete the account: click **Approve**, type the username (or the email for a parent account) to confirm, then confirm. **This cannot be undone.** You can only approve when the person's email is verified.
3. To refuse: click **Reject**, write a short reason, then click **Reject**.

Good to know:

- When an account is deleted, the person gets a **confirmation email**.
- Deletions made inside the app are done at once. They appear under **Completed**.
- After a deletion, the person's email stays on the request for **30 days**. Only Full Admins can see it. After that it is **erased automatically** for privacy.

## 8. Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first page after you log in. Full Admins see these cards:

- **Total accounts:** every account. Click it to open Users.
- **Adult / independent:** accounts for adults.
- **Parent accounts:** accounts made by parents.
- **Child profiles:** profiles that parents made for their children.
- **New registrations today:** new accounts since midnight. The card shows the time zone used, for example **"Since midnight, Kolkata time"** (India time).
- **Pending deletion requests:** requests waiting for you. It says **Needs review** when there are some, or **All clear** when there are none. Click it to open the Pending tab.

Below the cards you see charts (users by age group, new registrations, mode and language), the latest registrations and the latest admin actions.

## 9. Audit log (Full Admins only)

- Every admin action is recorded: logins, new admins, role changes, deletions and more.
- You can see who did what, and when.
- Nobody can change or delete these records.
- You can filter by date with **From** and **To**.

## 10. Safety tips

- Never share your password or your login code. Not even with other staff.
- Each person has their own admin account. Use **Add admin** for new staff.
- On a shared computer, always click **Log out** when you finish.
- Do not let your browser save your password on a shared computer.
- If you get a login code email you did not ask for, change your password at once and tell a Full Admin.
- When someone leaves the team, **Deactivate** them.
